Case 1 · SaaS mobile app

From manual Excel reports to hourly data.

Problem
Analysts downloaded data from each platform by hand and pasted it into one big Excel file. Keeping reports updated cost them hours.
Solution
Snowflake warehouse, Fivetran for fast ingestion, dbt models in 3 layers (raw, stage, mart) with quality checks, and Looker dashboards.
Result
Strategic decisions on fresh data within 1 month. Fivetran was later replaced by Airflow to cut costs, and new sources were added.

Case 3 · Fashion e-commerce · Shopify and Klaviyo

One source of truth for a fashion brand.

Goal
A growing women's fashion brand needed one source of truth across store, ads and email data.
Solution
Shopify, Klaviyo, Facebook Ads, Google Ads, TikTok and Google Analytics loaded with Stitch into BigQuery (migrated from Redshift), modeled in dbt Cloud, served in Looker.
Result
Up-to-date CAC, ROAS and margin. The team optimized ad spend, improved influencer ROI and boosted email performance.
